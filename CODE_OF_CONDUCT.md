# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). By participating, you agree to uphold it.

## Project-specific expectations

- Debate methods and evidence, not people.
- Do not publish confidential research, personal data, or client-identifying journey artifacts without permission. Examples must be fictional or explicitly licensed.
- Respect research participants: experience maps often describe customers and employees in vulnerable moments.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer, [@almazrobots](https://github.com/almazrobots). If the matter is sensitive, open an issue titled "Conduct report: private channel requested" without any details, and the maintainer will arrange a private channel. Reports are handled confidentially. Consequences follow the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines: correction, warning, temporary ban, or permanent ban.
