# Blueprint layer reference

## Line of interaction
Boundary where actor and service directly interact.

## Line of visibility
Separates activity visible to actor from backstage activity.

## Internal interaction
Boundary between core backstage delivery and supporting capability.

Do not obsess over literal line drawing. Preserve the semantic distinction.

## AI/automation as a service actor

Capture:
- decision rights;
- data provenance;
- confidence;
- human escalation;
- explainability needs;
- auditability;
- error containment.

The question is not "where can we add AI?" but "what service responsibility is being delegated, under what controls?"
