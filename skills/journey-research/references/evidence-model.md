# Evidence model

## Source type classes

### Direct qualitative
Interview, observation, diary, contextual inquiry.

### Direct quantitative
Survey, telemetry, analytics, experiment.

### Operational
Tickets, timestamps, quality records, process logs.

### Documentary
Policy, training, scripts, process documents.

### Stakeholder knowledge
Useful for hypothesis generation, not automatically actor evidence.

## Claim pattern

`[Actor] in [context] tends to [behavior/experience] because [supported explanation], affecting [outcome].`

Attach:
- source IDs;
- date;
- limitations;
- evidence state.

## Contradictions

Do not average contradictions away. Record:
- what conflicts;
- possible segmentation/context explanation;
- additional evidence needed.
