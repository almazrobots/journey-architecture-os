# Journey Workshop Facilitation

## Goal

Use workshops to create alignment, hypotheses, decisions, and ownership without turning group opinion into fake research.

## Core rule

**A stakeholder workshop can create a hypothesis map. It cannot, by itself, validate the customer's or employee's lived experience.**

## Before the workshop

Define:

- decision required;
- artifact to produce;
- evidence available;
- participants;
- decision owner;
- facilitator;
- timebox;
- pre-read;
- unresolved questions.

Also fix the decision rule (decider, consent, consensus, or vote) and collect independent pre-session input; see Steps 1 and 5 of `references/facilitation-method.md`.

## Participant mix

Include people who collectively understand:

- actor/customer/employee;
- frontstage;
- backstage operations;
- policy;
- data/systems;
- measurement;
- delivery constraints.

Invite real customers/employees only when research design, consent, and facilitation are appropriate.

## Workflow

### 1. Open with decision and evidence rules
Label every contribution `observed`, `inferred`, `hypothesis`, or `unknown`. `stakeholder-input` — everything participants know without a research or data source — supports `hypothesis` at most.

### 2. Align on scope
Actor, trigger, start, end, state, exclusions.

### 3. Diverge silently first
Reduce anchoring by having participants generate observations independently before discussion.

Use the evidence tags, silent-generation techniques, dissent log, and power-dynamics rules in `references/facilitation-method.md` Steps 2–4 and 7; convert outputs into registers within two working days (Step 8).

### 4. Build the journey
Prioritize actor goals/actions over internal process.

### 5. Attach evidence
Use evidence IDs or source notes.

### 6. Surface disagreement
Do not force consensus where evidence conflicts.

### 7. Diagnose
Identify:
- friction;
- workarounds;
- failures;
- moments candidates;
- evidence gaps.

### 8. Converge on decisions
What will be:
- researched;
- measured;
- redesigned;
- owned;
- deferred?

### 9. Close with activation
Assign owners and dates for unresolved evidence and decisions.

## Conventions

Follow `references/conventions.md` for IDs, evidence statuses, and register columns.

## Output contract

- workshop objective;
- agenda;
- participant roles;
- facilitation script;
- artifact template;
- decision log;
- evidence gaps;
- owner/action log.

## Quality gates

- evidence rules stated;
- decision owner known;
- silent divergence used for high-bias topics;
- dissent preserved;
- assumptions clearly labeled;
- post-workshop actions assigned.
