# Employee experience lenses

This is the canonical lens list for employee journey mapping. Lenses are questions to ask of each stage, not mandatory rows. Pick the two to four that explain the friction the decision is about; a map that fills every lens for every stage hides its findings.

| Lens | Look for | Typical evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Role clarity | Does the employee know what is expected, by when, and what "good" looks like? | Interviews, first-90-day check-ins, manager 1:1 notes (with consent) |
| Manager | Approvals, feedback, coaching, local interpretation of policy; the manager as a service touchpoint and a single point of failure | Approval timestamps, interviews, exit and pulse comments |
| Team and collaboration | Informal help, buddies, hidden knowledge, who the employee actually asks | Observation, diary studies, collaboration-tool patterns in aggregate |
| Culture and norms | Leadership behavior, inclusion, decision practices, psychological safety, unwritten rules | Interviews, listening surveys; treat single-team findings as local |
| Technology and tools | Access, interoperability, usability, automation, information availability, repeated data entry | Service-desk tickets, provisioning logs, usability tests |
| Physical and work environment | Workplace or remote setup, equipment, accessibility, safety, travel, scheduling, shift patterns | Facilities requests, observation, accessibility audits |
| Policy | Rules that are unclear, contradictory, or applied differently by location or manager | Policy documents (intended practice), case records (actual practice) |
| Organizational services | HR, IT, finance, facilities, legal, security, learning as delivery channels, with their handoffs | Ticket data, SLA reports, service blueprints |
| Capability, learning, and career | Skills, role readiness, access to expertise, growth paths | Learning records, skill assessments, interviews |
| Pay and benefits | Correct and on-time pay, understandable benefits, enrollment effort | Payroll error records, HR case data |
| Well-being and safety | Workload, stress, physical and psychological safety | Incident records, listening surveys; minimize and aggregate |
| Organizational change | Restructures, system migrations, policy changes that reset the journey mid-way | Change calendars, before/after comparisons |

Rules:

- Do not infer morale, engagement, or emotion from process data alone; a slow approval is `observed`, the frustration it causes is `hypothesis` until employees say so.
- Well-being, performance, health, and pay data are sensitive: use aggregates, remove names, and record the legal basis for use.
- For operational depth behind any lens, link a service blueprint instead of adding rows to the map.
