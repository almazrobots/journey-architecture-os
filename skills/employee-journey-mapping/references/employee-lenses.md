# Employee experience lenses

These are lenses, not mandatory rows.

## Cultural
Norms, leadership behavior, inclusion, decision practices, psychological and social context.

## Technological
Tools, access, interoperability, usability, automation, information availability.

## Physical / work environment
Workplace, remote environment, safety, equipment, accessibility, travel, scheduling.

## Manager
Clarity, feedback, approvals, coaching, local interpretation of policy.

## Organizational service
HR, IT, finance, facilities, legal, security, learning.

## Capability
Skills, learning, confidence, role readiness, access to expertise.

A journey may cut across all of these, but do not overload the map. Use linked service blueprints for operational depth.
